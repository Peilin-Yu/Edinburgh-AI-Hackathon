Questions

Good to know

Who sees my request?

Sellers who can meet it see what you want, your budget, deadline and area. Your name and contact details stay hidden until you accept an offer, and then go to that one seller only.

How are offers ranked?

Fit with your must-haves counts most (60%), then price against your budget (up to 30 points, dropping quickly once it goes over), then how soon it arrives or starts (up to 8 points): fit × 0.6 + price + speed. Nobody can pay to rank higher.

What are "Found online" results?

Real listings we found on other websites that match your request, with the price as listed and a link. Those sellers haven't seen your request, so you buy from them on their own site. In this prototype they're shown for the example requests only.

What does the AI do?

Claude turns your words into a clear request, matches it against sellers' catalogues, checks each offer against your must-haves, and runs autopilot rules for sellers who switch it on.

Is this live?

It's a working prototype for the Flip the Script hackathon. The AI and the Found online listings are real; the other sellers and buyers are simulated so you can see the whole flow.
